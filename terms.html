1. What ORE HAUNT is

ORE HAUNT is a premium intel app for ORE miners, published by Raaru. It presents public on-chain ORE round history as miner dossiers, a round explorer, replays and anomaly signals.

Independence. ORE HAUNT is an independent, unofficial companion app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or produced by the ORE protocol or its team.

Descriptive intel, not financial advice. HAUNT's signals are descriptive, not predictions — in the app's own words, they flag "unusual rounds, not predictions," and "nothing here is financial advice." The ORE draw is not predicted by HAUNT, and no pass, signal, dossier or replay in the app is a promise or prediction of any mining outcome, return, or profit. What you do with the record HAUNT shows you is your decision and your risk.

7. Chat — link-free rule and community moderation

Chat is a local preview in the current build (messages stay on your device; see the Privacy Policy, §3.3). The rules in this section state the standing rules for HAUNT chat. §7.1 states the link-free rule as shipped: the CH-14 hasLink gate is present and enforced in the shipped client. §7.2 is designed now and enforced server-side when the live chat transport launches — no flag, jury or mod-log surface exists in the local-preview build, and none is claimed to.

7.1 Link-free chat (CH-14)

  • No external links in HAUNT chat. A message containing a link cannot be sent at all. The composer blocks the send and shows: "Links aren't allowed in HAUNT chat — they put wallets at risk. Remove the link and post again."
  • The block covers, among the forms specified in CH-14: :// and scheme spellings (including obfuscated ones such as hxxp), www. prefixes, bare domains on the specified TLD list, known link shorteners, spelled-out / bracketed obfuscations of such domains, and email-shaped text.
  • It is not a ban on talking about the ecosystem: wallet addresses, round references (e.g. #438221), .skr handles and .sol names, decimals and version numbers are not links and are not blocked on that ground. Internal round / wallet references in chat are app navigation, not external links.
  • The gate never rewrites your message into a postable form: your text stays as you typed it, unsent, for you to edit.
  • Rationale, stated plainly: in a community whose members hold wallets, a clickable or postable external link is the drainer's delivery route. The rule is enforcement by design, not a preference about conversation.

7.2 Community-enforced moderation (CH-15) — applies with the live transport

  • Layer 0 — by design. Link-free chat (§7.1); one identity per device, bound at transport to the authenticated .skr (Seed Vault / Genesis-backed); and server posting rate ramps (launch values in the spec: newer identities post more slowly than established ones).
  • Layer 1 — community flags. Any member in good standing may flag a message that is not their own (one flag per identity per message). At 3 distinct identities flagging the same message, it auto-hides immediately pending review, showing "Hidden by community flags — under review." Flags are recorded with the flagger's identity and are never anonymous to the review process.
  • Layer 2 — jury review. Each auto-hidden message opens a case heard by a jury of 5, drawn at random from Founder Lifetime holders and high-reputation miners, excluding the author, the flaggers, and anyone currently muted or banned. Jurors decide restore / remove and, if remove, a sanction of none / 24h mute / 7-day channel ban. Quorum is 3 ballots in 48 hours; a tie or no quorum restores the message. A platform ban is never an ordinary ballot outcome: it needs a dedicated case and 4 of 5 jurors.
  • Layer 3 — transparency and appeal. Decided cases are posted to a public, read-only #mod-log channel (system-posted, showing the vote split — never which juror voted which way, and never flagger names). An author may appeal a removal or ban outcome once, within 7 days, to a fresh jury; the original disposition stands while the appeal is heard, and the appeal verdict is final for the community layers.
  • Sanctions ladder. Message removal → 24h mute → 7-day channel ban → platform ban, applied in that graduated order through the jury process above, except under the operator floor below.
  • The operator floor — no vote, no appeal. Some content is never community business: CSAM, credible threats of violence, and doxxing (posting another person's private personal data). The operator removes it immediately, bypassing flags, jury and appeal of the removal itself, may platform-ban on the spot, and may refer out where the law expects it. No jury verdict, flag count or appeal can restore floor-removed content. The action is still recorded in the mod log, in the form: "🛡️ Removed by the operator — policy floor (no community vote)."
