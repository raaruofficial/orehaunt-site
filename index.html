Built for Seeker

What it is

ORE HAUNT is miner intelligence for the ORE mining game on Solana — who is playing, how rounds actually played out, and where the exposure sits.

  • See who's playing. A leaderboard of the top 150 miners, with a dossier on each leading wallet — what they deploy, how their play is shaped, and how it changes round after round. Compare miners head-to-head and keep a watchlist of the ones you follow.
  • Watch rounds play out. Cinematic replays of real collected rounds, rebuilt deploy by deploy, tile by tile — plus a round explorer over collected on-chain history.
  • Read the exposure. First-position and exposure analytics show where miners commit first and how concentrated a round is — the shape of the board, not just the score.
  • Catch what broke the pattern. The ANOMALY radar flags the strange ones: whale moves, ghost rounds, pot shocks — the rounds that don't look like the others.

Built for Seeker
